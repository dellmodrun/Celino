THE MANIFESTO

THE MOST VALUABLE KNOWLEDGE
IS THE KNOWLEDGE THAT CANNOT BE SHARED.

Agent networks are trained on what the public internet will surrender. The deeper intelligence remains elsewhere: internal research, private repositories, operating history, specialist datasets, personal context, and permissions that should never leave their owner.

Celino makes that closed knowledge useful without making it public. A private source becomes a sealed capability. Other agents may ask it to perform a bounded task, but they cannot inspect, download, memorize, or inherit the source behind the result.

Do not move the secret to the agent.
Move the question to the secret.

WHY LIQUIDITY

LIQUIDITY IS THE ABILITY
TO MOVE WITHOUT GIVING UP OWNERSHIP.

LOCKED

A private archive can help one owner.

≠
EXPOSED

A copied dataset can help everyone but protect no one.

→
CELINO

A sealed capability can serve many requests while the source remains in place.

Celino calls this capability liquidity: the useful behavior of private knowledge becomes addressable, composable and payable, while custody and disclosure remain constrained by the owner. The liquid object is not the data. It is the right to ask one bounded question.

BUILD SURFACE

A CAPABILITY IS A CONTRACT
WITH A SMALL SURFACE.

Owners publish schemas and policies, not source material.

capability "private-code-risk" {
  accepts     = ["text/code", "sha256"]
  returns     = ["verdict", "category", "confidence", "proof"]
  source      = sealed("vault://owner/security-corpus")
  disclosure  = deny(["rules", "samples", "memory", "credentials"])
  budget      = compose(window: "24h", limit: 0.72)
  settlement  = after(valid_output && valid_proof)
}
