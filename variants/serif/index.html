I · the regression nobody names

crypto un-learned the oldest trick in money

A crypto balance is a single number that divides forever — eighteen decimals, split it as fine as you like. This is treated as sophistication. It is the opposite. It is precisely the state money sat in before Lydia struck the first standardized coin around 600 BC and turned "weigh this dust" into "count these pieces."

Counting beat weighing. The denomination was the leap. Crypto quietly walked it back to dust on a scale.

Nacreum re-learns it. Money should come in bills again — discrete, faced, countable — and on a chain, those bills can do something paper never could: prove their own history, split and recombine without a teller, and never quietly print or vanish a single unit of value.

II · the unit

what a note is

Not a balance. A note. Four things, all real, all on-chain:

  • a face value — the denomination it wears: a 1, a 20, a 500, a 1,000,000;
  • a serial — its own number, from #1 to #1,000,000,000, unforgeable and unlosable;
  • a face — nation, portrait, palette, series, all computed from the serial, so no two look alike;
  • two verbs — it can be broken into change and fused back into a bill.

Your wallet stops being a number and becomes a billfold: a fistful of different notes, and no two people's are the same.

III · the two operations

break & fuse — try it, watch the total hold

Two on-chain verbs, and one iron rule between them:

  • break — split a big note into change; fresh serials are minted, each its own face;
  • fuse — melt a fistful of change into one larger note;
  • conservation — neither verb ever changes the total face value in the room. No printing. No burning by sleight of hand. That discipline is the whole point, and it's the part that has to be proven in code — not asserted in a slogan.

So here is the proof, running live in your browser. Select a note and break it; select a few and fuse them. Watch the running total: it does not move.

V · the catalog

a field guide to the rare notes

Most of a billion are ordinary small bills. The generator seeds a scarce few on purpose — the same three things note-collectors have chased for two centuries:

grade~sharewhat makes it prized
common~92%standard denomination & palette
uncommon~6%higher denomination, a scarce series letter
rare~1.5%1,000+ face value, or a fancy serial (radar / solid / ladder)
legendary~0.05%a 1,000,000 face value; star-note series
errora scarce fewa misprint: inverted seal, wrong-colour pass, offset print

In the paper world a genuine misprint or the serial 00000001 sells for many times face. Here they are printed, on purpose, into specific serials — and because the seed and odds are published before launch, anyone can audit the whole census. Two behaviours organise themselves without a marketing budget: bragging and hunting.

VI · provenance

a note remembers every hand

Today's money is amnesiac. A dollar carries nothing of where it has been; that amnesia is exactly what makes it fungible. A Nacreum note is different: it carries its own lineage — every wallet it passed through, on-chain and verifiable. The more a note has travelled, the more it has seen, and the more a collector wants it.

Fungibility is just amnesia. Nacreum trades a little of it away, on purpose, so a note can have a life instead of only a value.
VII · the retired ledger

where fused-out serials go

When notes are fused, their serials are not destroyed into nothing — they are retired to the ledger with the face value they wore and the count of hands they held. The retired ledger is a readable record: which note travelled furthest, which million-note was broken down to buy something small, which error note survived un-fused the longest. Value is always conserved; only the shape of it changes.

VIII · the long view

five thousand years, and where Nacreum sits

Money keeps changing its body and never its job. Every era asked one question: what stands behind this, and can I trust it without asking a stranger?

Crypto solved trust and, in the same motion, dropped the denomination. Nacreum is the small correction — the note, put back on the new rails. Not a new kind of money; the oldest thing the newest money forgot.

IX · the claim

why this is actually new, and what's hard about it

Plenty of coins have a picture. What Nacreum has is a primitive: money that is denominated at the protocol layer, with break and fuse as first-class operations. That is not a skin on top of a balance — it changes what a unit is.

  • the honest hard part — break/fuse must conserve total face value under adversarial use: no minting rare serials by churning, no rounding leaks, no double-spend across a split. That is real program design, and it is the reason this is a build and not a poster.
  • scale — a billion faced, serialised, semi-fungible notes have to be cheap to hold and move; that is state-compression and careful accounting, not a JPEG folder.
  • verifiability — every note is recomputable from its serial by anyone, so the whole census can be audited without trusting us.

The workbench in §III is a browser model of exactly this discipline. The on-chain version is the same rule, enforced by code.

XII · questions

straight answers

if it's semi-fungible, is it still a normal coin?

At the base, yes: one token is one unit of face value and trades on pump.fun like anything else. Notes are the layer above — how tokens are bundled, faced, and serialised. You can hold it flat; you just can't un-see the denomination once it's back.

what stops someone farming rare serials by breaking and fusing forever?

Conservation plus rate-limits: break/fuse can never increase total face value, and serial assignment is bound so churn doesn't manufacture legendaries. Exact rules ship in the spec — this is the core engineering, covered in §IX.

where do a billion note images live?

Nowhere. Each is computed from its serial on demand. Zero storage, forgery-proof.

why solana / pump.fun?

Cheap blocks, fast settlement, a fair launch. A billion tiny notes need exactly that.

is this financial advice?

No. It's a memecoin with a real mechanism and an argument inside it. Read the small print like an adult.
